Privacy notice

This notice covers Umbrella HQ Home Assistant, a privately operated, non-commercial Home Assistant installation, and this informational website. It is written for the owner who grants the installation access to their Google account, and for anyone who wants to know what the app name on a Google permissions screen refers to.

What the installation is

Umbrella HQ Home Assistant is a Home Assistant instance run by a private individual for their own household. It is not a public service and has no customers. It is independent of Google and of the Home Assistant project.

What data may be processed

When the owner links their Google account, the installation uses Google's Smart Device Management API and Google Cloud Pub/Sub to connect the owner's Google Nest devices to Home Assistant. Depending on the permissions the owner grants, the following may be processed:

  • Device identifiers, names, and room assignments for the owner's Nest devices.
  • Device state, such as temperature, humidity, mode, and connectivity.
  • Device events, such as motion, sound, doorbell, or person detection notifications.
  • Thermostat controls, when the owner uses Home Assistant to change a setpoint or mode.
  • Camera streams and event media, when the owner grants camera access and views or records them through Home Assistant.

Which of these apply depends on the devices the owner has and the scopes the owner accepts on Google's consent screen.

Google credentials

The installation never sees the owner's Google password. Sign-in happens on Google's own pages. Google issues OAuth grants and tokens that the installation stores and uses to access the permitted devices. The owner can withdraw these grants at any time from their Google account or from the Google Nest partner connections settings.

Where data is kept

Data flows to a Home Assistant instance the owner controls. Home Assistant may store integration configuration, state history, event media, and backups according to how the owner has configured it. The owner decides how long history is retained, whether media is saved, and where backups go. This notice does not promise a specific retention period, because those settings are the owner's to change.

How data is used

Data is used for household automation, monitoring, and control within the owner's home. It is not used for advertising, commercial profiling, or sale. The integration relies on Google services and the owner-controlled Home Assistant instance. Any additional sharing depends on integrations and storage destinations the owner enables.

Google's role

Google processes data on its side under its own terms and privacy policy. This notice does not cover what Google does with data from Nest devices or Google accounts.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the installation's access by revoking it in their Google account settings or in the Google Nest partner connections settings. Removing the Google Nest integration from Home Assistant stops that integration. Previously stored history, media, and backups must be deleted separately wherever they are retained. Data that remains with Google is governed by Google's own policies and deletion tools.

This website

This site is a static informational page hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-up, login, payment, comment, or data-collection forms, and it loads no analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process standard access logs such as IP addresses and request details; see GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles that.

Contact

Questions about this notice go through the public issue tracker at github.com/mimen/home-assistant-app-info/issues. Issues are public, so do not include addresses, account details, device identifiers, or other private information.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the installation changes. The current version is always the one published at this address.
